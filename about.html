About Roasted

We are a little obsessed with golden edges.

Roasted is an independent home-cooking site about one technique: oven roasting.

We think it is the most forgiving and rewarding way to cook at home. Our guides explain the temperatures, timings and small habits that turn a tray of vegetables or a simple chicken into something you look forward to. Our tools give fast answers when you are standing at the oven.
